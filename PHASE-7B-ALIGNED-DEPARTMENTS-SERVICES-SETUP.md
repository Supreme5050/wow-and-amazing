# Wow & Amazing — Phase 7B

This update reduces the public-facing departments from six to four:

1. Gadgets & Accessories
2. Food & Catering
3. Property & Home Services
4. Media Services & Gadget Rentals

The underlying product category IDs remain unchanged, so existing products, orders, payments, rental listings, stock, and admin records are preserved. The storefront groups the existing categories into four customer-friendly hubs.

## Service areas

The public Services page is aligned to:

- Food & Catering
- Property & Home Services
